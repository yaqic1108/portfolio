Robot Mechanism

Yaqinator (Amp System)

A mechanical conveyor system designed to help score points at two different levels during a robotics competition.

OnShape 3D Printing Mechanical Design

My role
Led group of 5+ people in prototyping, designing, and building the mechansim.

Tools
OnShape · 3D Printing · Mechanical Design · Laser Cutting

Timeline
September 2023 — May 2024

Objective

Led a team in building a mechanism to help score point during the FRC 2024 Cresendo Season. The mechanism would need to intake a foam ring game piece with a diameter of 14 inches and lift it to a desired height to score.

Prototyping

The first prototype was made with pipes wih rubber wrapped in a spiral to increase grip and alumnimum tubing to test concept and functionality.

Second prototype was made with more persision. In order to test at various scoring angles, the plates were designed with multiple mounting holes. We also descreased the diameter of the rollers by using hex shafts instead to test the limit of the mechanism.

Integration

The final design consisted of two polycarbonate plates, 3D printed roller hubs and gears, and 4 polycarbonate rollers.

An IR sensor was also added in order to automates increase efficiency of scoring the game piece.

Results

Mechanism was successfully integrated into the robot and helped score points during the competition, with < 1% failure rate during the competition.
